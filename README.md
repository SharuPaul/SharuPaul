## Hi, I'm Sharu Paul 👋

Bioinformatics Scientist at the Bioinformatics Facility, Iowa State University, where I develop computational methods and analysis pipelines across genomics, transcriptomics, proteomics, and deep learning.

I collaborate with researchers from academia, government, and industry on projects spanning different biological systems, experimental approaches, and computational challenges.

### 🔭 I’m currently
* Investigating host responses to infection and characterizing immune cell populations using **single-cell RNA-seq** across multiple species. [View analysis code →](https://github.com/SharuPaul/Porcine_SingleCell_RNAseq_sal_FS27)
* Analyzing **proteomics data** to study the effects of heat stress, environmental contaminants, and carcinogens on reproductive health.
* Developing **transformer-based models** for DNA sequence analysis.
* Building **reproducible bioinformatics workflows** to automate sequencing data processing, quantification, and differential expression analysis. [Explore RNA-seq pipeline →](https://github.com/SharuPaul/RNASeq)
* Writing  and conducting workshops for researchers early in their bioinformatics journey
* Developing [bioinformatics tutorials and workbooks](https://github.com/ISUgenomics/bioinformatics-workbook) and conducting hands-on workshops to help researchers build computational skills.

### 🌱 I’m interested in
* Applying Deep Neural Networks in medicine
* Single-cell and spatial omics technologies
* Developing scalable and reproducible bioinformatics tools
